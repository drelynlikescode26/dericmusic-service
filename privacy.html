Privacy

Your Project Details.

Here’s what happens when you contact me through this site.

Project Inquiries

The form asks for your name, email, the service you’re interested in, and your project details. Music links, budget, deadline, and social profile are optional. I use this information to reply, discuss your project, and arrange the work if we move forward.

Formspree processes and stores the submission and forwards it to my music inbox. Your inquiry does not subscribe you to a mailing list. Please share only the information needed to discuss your project. Formspree’s privacy policy explains its processing.

Where The Inquiry Came From

The site carries campaign labels, such as the ad campaign name, through to your inquiry. When available, it also includes the referring website’s domain and the page you submitted from. Campaign labels are kept in this browser tab’s session storage so they follow you between pages. Your name, email, and project text are not saved in browser storage.

Optional Analytics And Advertising Measurement

Google Analytics and Meta conversion tracking are currently inactive until the relevant account IDs are connected. If enabled, they load only after you allow measurement. Conversion events contain the selected service and an anonymous inquiry reference where needed, not your contact details or project text. You can change your choice through Measurement preferences in the footer. The inquiry form works if you decline.

The site honors Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals for these optional tools. When enabled with your permission, the tools may use cookies and receive device and network information. See Google’s privacy policy and Meta’s privacy policy.

Audio, Artwork, And Checkout

Audio files and site images load as you use the site. Fonts are served by Google Fonts; selected release artwork comes from SoundCloud. Following a marketplace, music platform, or Stripe checkout link takes you to that provider, which has its own privacy practices. Stripe processes payment details.

Questions Or Deletion Requests

Contact contact@dericmusic.com to ask about your inquiry details, request a correction, or request deletion. I keep inquiry information for project communication and records; you can ask what is held about your inquiry.
