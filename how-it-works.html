Before you book

A Few Answers.

Do I have to book before we talk?

No. Send a project inquiry and we’ll discuss what you need. An inquiry does not take a payment or reserve a booking.

What exactly do I get?

Writing orders include the hook or song and a reference vocal to guide your recording. Vocal orders include my performance on your track. We confirm the file formats and any separate vocal tracks before you pay.

What happens after I send an inquiry?

I review your idea and reply to the email you provide. We’ll talk through the project before you book. Sending an inquiry does not take a payment or reserve a slot.

How do revisions work?

We agree on the number of revision rounds and what they cover before you book. If you need something beyond that, we’ll discuss it first.

How long does it take?

Custom hooks and features typically take 2 business days. Full songwriting and full records take 3 to 4 business days. We confirm timing before booking; the delivery period starts once payment and the project brief are received.

Can I release the reference vocal?

Reference vocals are there to help you hear the writing. They are not release vocals.

What does ghostwriting mean?

You can release the song under your name. Credit is appreciated, but it is always your call.

What if Deric is performing on my record?

Credits, splits, and release terms are agreed before work starts.

Is payment secure?

Yes. Payments are processed securely through Stripe. Your card details never touch this site.
